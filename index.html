Features

Everything you need to act on feedback

Powerful essentials, none of the clutter.

Easy Feedback Submission

A guided form with categories and a live character counter makes sharing feedback quick and effortless.

Ratings and Reviews

Capture a clear 1–5 star rating alongside written comments for context that numbers alone can’t give.

Feedback Management

Search, filter, review and organise every submission from a single, tidy admin inbox.

Status Tracking

Move feedback from Pending to Reviewed to Resolved so nothing slips through the cracks.

Analytics and Reports

Visual breakdowns by category, rating and status, plus one-click CSV export for reporting.

User-Friendly Dashboard

A clean, role-based workspace — users see their submissions, admins see the full picture.
