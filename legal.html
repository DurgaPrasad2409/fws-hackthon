Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we store

FeedbackHub stores the account details you enter (name, username and a salted, hashed password) and the feedback you submit (name, category, rating, comment and status).

Where it is stored

This version runs entirely in your browser. Data is kept in your browser’s local storage and is not sent to any server or shared with third parties.

Your control

You can clear your data at any time by clearing this site’s storage in your browser. Administrators can delete individual feedback entries.
